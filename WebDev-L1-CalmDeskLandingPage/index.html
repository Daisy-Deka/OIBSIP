What you get

Three things, done properly — not a whole productivity suite.

CalmDesk stays small on purpose. Every feature exists to get you through a study block without burning out before the exam does.

⏱

Study timer

Set focused blocks with breaks built in from the start, so you stop working past the point of actually retaining anything.

◐

Mood check-ins

A ten-second tap before and after each session tracks how you're really doing — not just how many hours you logged.

〜

Breathing breaks

Guided sixty-second resets between sessions, built from breathing techniques counselors actually recommend.

How it works

Three steps, every session.

1

Set a session

Pick a subject and a focus length that matches your energy today, not an arbitrary hour.

2

Check in

Rate your stress before you start. No journaling, no forms — just a tap.

3

Breathe, then go

A short guided breath resets you before the timer even starts counting.
